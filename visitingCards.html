The Brief

Small card,
big personality

A visiting card is often the only physical object a brand puts into someone's hands. The design needs to work instantly: the eye should land on the most important thing first, and every element after that should build trust.

For Artful Bytes, the brief was refined luxury — a navy textured card with the AB monogram foil-embossed on the front, and a clean information layout with flowing gold line-work on the reverse. The tagline "Where creativity meets strategy" grounds the aesthetic.

For Flame & Fork, the goal was warmth and appetite — a kraft-texture card with the fiery fork mark, rich brown and amber tones, and a golden info panel on the reverse highlighting the restaurant's three brand pillars: Bold · Authentic · Freshly Made.
