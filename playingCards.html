The Brief

Designing a deck
from scratch

The challenge was to create a complete, print-ready playing card deck under the Artful Bytes brand identity — 54 unique cards, two distinct colourways, custom suit illustrations, and bespoke face-card characters.

The result is a deck that reads as premium and bold: a deep crimson/gold palette for the face-up side that feels festive and luxurious, paired with an ivory/magenta pinstripe reverse that gives a cleaner, more classical feel.

Every suit symbol was redrawn as a hanging, layered illustration — hearts, diamonds, clubs and spades each suspended from a vertical line, giving each card a distinctive decorative rhythm.
